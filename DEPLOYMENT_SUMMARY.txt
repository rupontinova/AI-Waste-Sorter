Free Tier Limitations:
- Render: Spins down after 15 min (30s cold start)
- Vercel: 100GB bandwidth/month (plenty for most projects)
- Hugging Face: No limitations on free tier

Cold Start Explained:
- On Render free tier, your backend sleeps after 15 min of inactivity
- First request after sleep takes ~30 seconds to wake up
- Subsequent requests are fast
- Solution: Use Hugging Face (no cold starts) or upgrade Render ($7/month)
