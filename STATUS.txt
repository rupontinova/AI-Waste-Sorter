═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
💡 KEY INSIGHTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Architecture Decision:
  Instead of training from scratch, we used a pre-trained YOLOv8
  model and implemented intelligent class remapping. This provides:
    • Faster development (hours vs. days/weeks)
    • Good baseline accuracy
    • Easy customization
    • No need for training data initially

Port 5001 vs 5000:
  macOS reserves port 5000 for AirPlay/AirTunes. To avoid
  conflicts, we use port 5001 for the Flask backend.

Decoupled Architecture:
  Separating frontend and backend allows:
    • Independent scaling
    • Multiple frontends (web, mobile, IoT)
    • Easy deployment to different platforms
    • Clear separation of concerns
